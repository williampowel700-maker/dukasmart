Understand the figures

  • Sales record the full value of goods sold, including credit sales.
  • Cash received records money actually collected. A later debt repayment is cash received, not another sale.
  • Gross profit = sales value − cost of goods sold.
  • Estimated net operating profit = gross profit − operating expenses entered in DukaSmart.
  • Cash movement = cash received − operating expenses − stock purchases. This is not your total cash balance.
  • Figures are estimates and exclude taxes, loan interest, owner withdrawals, depreciation and costs you have not entered.
